How these terms are presented

Index provides these Terms of Use for transparency and review. Students are not required to click an “I agree” checkbox or button in order to enter the application. Formal school use remains subject to the applicable school or district review, privacy, security, accessibility, consent, and instructional requirements.

1. Educational purpose

Index is an independent study platform providing study organization, notes, study sets, practice, games, rewards, teacher-hosted live games, and AI tutoring.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use Index for lawful educational and personal study purposes.
  • Use respectful names and communication in shared spaces and live games.
  • Do not disrupt the service, attack the server, bypass access controls, or attempt to defeat school or network security.
  • Do not impersonate another person or use another person’s credentials.
  • Do not enter passwords, student ID numbers, home addresses, phone numbers, or other sensitive student information into notes, study sets, or AI chats.

3. Academic integrity and AI

The AI Tutor is designed to teach concepts and explain reasoning. Do not use it to cheat on an active graded test, quiz, exam, or other assessment. Follow the teacher, school, and district rules that apply to your assignment.

AI responses can be wrong. Verify important facts and calculations against class materials, teachers, or reliable sources.

4. Student safety and conduct

Index expects respectful behavior. Harassment, bullying, threats, sexual content, hateful content, and harmful or illegal instructions are not permitted. Live-game names and titles may be filtered or rejected.

5. Accounts and rewards

Accounts are optional for core study features. When an account is used, Index may save profile information and reward progress such as XP, coins, Battle Pass progress, cosmetics, and related settings. Virtual rewards have no cash value. Account passwords are processed using server-side password hashing.

6. Live games

Teacher-hosted live games can process temporary room, display-name, answer, and score data while a game is active. Hosts and players are responsible for following applicable school rules and host instructions.

7. School and district requirements

Index does not replace a school’s acceptable-use rules, classroom instructions, digital-tool review, privacy requirements, or consent process. A public website being accessible does not mean it is approved for formal classroom use.

8. Availability and changes

Index may change, contain errors, or become temporarily unavailable. No guarantee is made that every feature works on every school-managed device, browser, or network. These terms may be updated as the application changes.

Index is an independent educational project.
