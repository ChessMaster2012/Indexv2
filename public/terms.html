Terms of Use

Local school-use context

1. Educational purpose

Index is an independent educational study platform. It provides study organization, practice, games, AI tutoring, rewards, and teacher-hosted live games.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use Index for lawful educational and personal study purposes.
  • Use respectful names and communication in live games.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt the service, bypass access controls, or interfere with school security systems.
  • Do not upload or enter sensitive personal information into study content or AI messages.

3. Academic integrity

The AI Tutor is intended to teach and explain. Students must follow their teacher's and school/district rules for AI use and must not use the tutor to cheat on an active graded assessment.

4. AI and generated content

AI-generated explanations can be incorrect. Students should verify important information with class materials, teachers, and reliable sources.

5. Accounts and rewards

Accounts are optional for core study features. When a student signs in, Index can save XP, coins, Battle Pass progress, owned cosmetics, equipped cosmetics, and related profile progress to that account so rewards persist across sessions and server restarts. XP, coins, Battle Pass levels, packs, and cosmetics are virtual features with no cash value. Daily earning limits may apply.

6. Live games

Teacher-hosted live games may process temporary display names, answers, scores, and room information. Students should use appropriate display names and follow the host's instructions.

7. School and district rules

Index does not replace school, classroom, device, acceptable-use, privacy, or digital-tool approval requirements. If your selected district requires a tool to be reviewed or approved, Index should not be treated as approved merely because it is available online.

8. Availability

Index is provided as an independent project and may change, be unavailable, or contain errors. No guarantee is made that every feature will work on every school-managed device or network.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated as Index changes. The effective date is shown above.
