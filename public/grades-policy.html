What custom assignments are

Custom assignments are what-if numbers only. They are not your grade.
  • You can add an assignment or change a score to see how a grade could move. Index does the math on your device.
  • Nothing you add or change is sent to StudentVUE, Canvas, your school or your teacher. Your official grade only changes when your teacher changes it.
  • Added and changed scores are marked "What-if" and the calculator shows a notice while they are in use. Use "Undo all my changes" to go back to the real numbers.
  • What-if data is saved in your browser only. Index's servers do not store it.

Rules for using them

  • Do not present what-if grades as real. Do not show edited numbers or screenshots to a teacher, parent, guardian, coach, scholarship committee or anyone else as your actual grade. Altering or falsifying grades or records is an academic-integrity and conduct violation in every district listed below, and can lead to discipline.
  • Do not edit your real record. Index cannot change grades in StudentVUE. Do not try to make it, and do not use the tool to get into anyone else's account.
  • Use your own account only. Sign in to StudentVUE with your own Student ID and password. Do not enter another student's or a staff member's login.
  • Use it for planning. Good uses: "What do I need on the final to keep a B?", "What if I turn in the missing homework?"
  • Grade weights and rounding rules differ by teacher and school. Treat results as an estimate and check with your teacher.

Your StudentVUE login

  • By default your password is sent straight to your school's StudentVUE site and is not stored.
  • Gold, Platinum and Diamond members can choose "Keep me connected". Then Index stores the login encrypted (AES-256-GCM) so grades load fresh when the Grades tab opens. You can turn this off at any time with "Disconnect", which deletes it.
  • Check your district's rules on third-party access to student records before connecting. If your district or a parent asks you not to connect, do not.
  • If a district turns off outside access to StudentVUE, import stops working and you can enter classes by hand instead.

Students under 18

Grades are education records. Students under 18 should use this feature with their parent or guardian's knowledge where the district requires it. Index does not sell or share grade data, and does not use it for ads.
