Privacy & data

Index is designed to collect as little information as possible.

  • Your school district choice is saved only on this device.
  • Index does not need your exact location.
  • You can use study tools and live games without an account.
  • If you sign in, only information needed for sign-in and your optional display name are stored.
  • Index does not ask for your school name, grade, student ID, home address, or phone number for personalization.
  • Do not put sensitive personal information into AI Tutor messages.

Important: Index is an independent project and is not an official MCPS service or endorsement.

Student Safety & Conduct

Index is designed as a learning tool, not a social network.

  • Use respectful names and language. Bullying, harassment, threats, hate-based content, and sexual content are not allowed.
  • Do not post another person's private information or use Index to embarrass, threaten, or impersonate someone.
  • Use AI to learn. Do not use it to cheat on active tests, quizzes, or other graded assessments.
  • Use Live Game only for appropriate educational activities and follow your teacher's instructions and MCPS device rules.
  • Do not use Index to bypass school security, filtering, account controls, or other technology restrictions.
  • Index is an independent student project and is not an official MCPS service, endorsement, or approved MCPS online digital tool.

These safeguards are intended to align the site with the conduct principles published by MCPS; they are not an MCPS approval or legal certification.
