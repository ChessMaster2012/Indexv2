Purpose

Index is a student-facing study platform for independent practice and teacher-led instruction. It combines course organization, lessons, notes, an AI tutor, study-set creation, solo games, rewards, and live quiz games.

Review status: Index does not represent itself as approved, contracted, or endorsed by the selected school district. This page is a local-context review document to help educators and families find the applicable district guidance.

Feature overview

🏠 DashboardStudy shortcuts, progress, notes, study sets, and local-context information.
📚 CoursesOrganizes supported academic subjects and course-focused study activities.
🎓 LearnGuided topic learning with explanations and checkpoints.
💬 AI TutorConversational academic help designed to explain concepts and give hints.
📝 NotesType or paste notes and upload supported files from a computer.
🗂️ Study SetsReusable flashcards and quizzes for practice.
🕹️ GamesSolo study games built from study sets.
🏆 RewardsXP, Battle Pass progression, coins, cosmetic avatars, and packs.
⚡ Live GameReal-time quiz rooms with room codes, timing, leaderboards, and player limits.

Student safety and conduct

  • Live-game names and room titles are sanitized and checked against conduct filters.
  • The AI tutor has server-side safety and academic-integrity guardrails.
  • The tutor is designed not to provide direct answers to active graded tests, quizzes, or exams.
  • The tutor is not intended for sexual content, harassment, threats, harmful instructions, or bypassing school security or rules.
  • Students are told not to enter sensitive personal information into AI messages.

Data minimization

The application does not request school name, grade, student ID, home address, or phone number for personalization. County/district selection is stored locally on the device. Most study content is stored in browser local storage rather than sent to the application server.
